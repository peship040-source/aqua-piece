STAGES (star2/star3 are score thresholds for bonus stars):
1: score, target 1000, moves 30, star2 1400, star3 1800

Export STAGES and stageDef(id). Also export earnedStars(def, game): 0 if not won, else 1 + (score>=star2) + (score>=star3).

2) Engine (src/game/engine.ts):
- Game gains collected: number[] (length 6, per-color cleared counts, starts 0) and stageId: number.
- stageFor(def, random): Game from a StageDef (moves=def.moves; keep game.goal = def.target for score stages; for collect stages goal may stay def.target too, but win judgment must use def). Keep stage(random) = stageFor(STAGES[0], random).
- Count every cleared tile's color during resolve (normal clears, special-activated clears, chain clears) into a per-turn clearedByColor, thread it through resolve/resolveBoard returns, and accumulate into game.collected in playTurn.
- status(game, def?): win when score-type score>=target, or collect-type collected[targetColor]>=target; lost when moves==0 and not won; else playing. Without def, resolve it from game.stageId so existing call sites keep working.
